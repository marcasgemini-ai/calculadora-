# Landing Metabomen +40 · Prompts de imágenes para ChatGPT (English)

## Paso a paso
1. Abrí un **chat nuevo** en ChatGPT por cada imagen.
2. Adjuntá lo que dice cada prompt (casi siempre: **Archivo A · foto del frasco Metabomen** de frente, bien iluminada).
3. Pegá el prompt y enviá.
4. Si el frasco sale distinto: `Replace the bottle with the exact bottle from the image I attached. Same label, same proportions. Keep everything else identical.`
5. Descargá, subí a Shopify (Contenido > Archivos), copiá la URL y pegala en la variable que dice cada prompt.

## 1 · HERO (bloque 01 · `img_hero`)
**Adjuntar:** nada (no lleva frasco).
**Formato:** horizontal 3:2. En celular se recorta al centro, por eso el hombre va en el centro.

```
Create a hyper-realistic horizontal 3:2 photograph, shot on a 50mm lens, natural late-afternoon light.

An Argentine man aged 48, salt-and-pepper hair, short neat grey beard, natural skin with real texture and expression lines, fit but realistic body, wearing a fitted plain black t-shirt. He stands outdoors on a city balcony in Buenos Aires with blurred buildings and trees behind him, looking straight at the camera with a calm, confident half smile. He looks healthy, rested and youthful for his age.
Composition: the man centered in the frame from mid-chest up, lots of soft out-of-focus background on both sides, the lower third of the image slightly darker (text will be placed there).
Color: warm natural tones with deep shadows, subtle cinematic contrast, no heavy color grading.

No text, no logos, no watermark. Real skin, not airbrushed, not a fashion model.
```
