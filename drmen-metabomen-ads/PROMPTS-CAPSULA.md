# Prompts · Cápsula Metabomen flotando (fondo transparente)

Cómo usarlos
- Abrí un chat nuevo en ChatGPT, subí la foto de tus cápsulas reales y pegá el prompt. Así copia el color y la forma exactos.
- Si sale con fondo blanco: "Same image, export as PNG with a fully transparent background (alpha channel), no white, no checkerboard."
- Si no sale transparente: Canva › Quitar fondo, o remove.bg.

---

## 1 · Cápsula sola flotando (la principal)

```
Use the attached photo only as a reference for the capsule's exact color, size and shape. Create an ultra-premium, hyper-realistic 3D product render of ONE single two-piece hard gelatin capsule, size 0, oblong with perfectly rounded ends, both halves the same deep, rich crimson red (close to #C4161C), fully opaque, with a smooth glossy lacquered finish. The seam where the cap overlaps the body is crisp and clearly visible as a fine, slightly raised ring, about 40% from one end.

The capsule floats in mid-air, weightless, tilted diagonally at about 35 degrees, with a slight rotation toward the camera so we see a hint of depth and the rounded end in perspective. Centered in the frame, occupying roughly 70% of the canvas width, with even empty space around it.

Lighting: high-end pharmaceutical / luxury cosmetic studio lighting. A large soft key light from the upper left creates a long, elegant specular highlight running along the top of the capsule; a cooler thin rim light on the right edge separates the silhouette; soft fill from below so the underside shows deep wine-red tones, never black. Subtle internal color gradation from bright cherry red in the highlights to dark burgundy in the shadows. Clean reflections, no hot spots, no noise.

Detail: microscopic realism, perfectly smooth surface with a very faint natural gelatin sheen, razor-sharp edges, 8K detail, photorealistic, product-photography quality, like an Apple or premium supplement brand campaign.

Background: completely transparent background, PNG with alpha channel, isolated object with clean anti-aliased edges. No background color, no floor, no surface, no cast shadow, no reflection on the ground, no drop shadow, no glow halo, no checkerboard pattern.

Strictly no text, no logos, no letters printed on the capsule, no other capsules, no pills, no hands, no props. Square 1:1, 2048 x 2048 px.
```

## 2 · Tres cápsulas flotando (composición)

```
Use the attached photo only as a reference for the capsules' exact color, size and shape. Create an ultra-premium, hyper-realistic 3D product render of THREE identical two-piece hard gelatin capsules, size 0, oblong with rounded ends, both halves deep crimson red (close to #C4161C), fully opaque, glossy lacquered finish, with a crisp visible seam ring where the cap overlaps the body.

The three capsules float in mid-air in a dynamic, balanced composition: one large capsule in the foreground tilted at about 30 degrees and perfectly sharp; one slightly smaller behind it to the upper right, tilted the opposite way; one smaller at the lower left, slightly rotated toward the camera. Each capsule at a different angle so the arrangement feels alive and weightless, like a premium supplement campaign. Very subtle depth of field: foreground capsule tack-sharp, the back ones only a touch softer. Group centered, filling about 80% of the canvas.

Lighting: luxury studio lighting. Large soft key light from the upper left with long elegant specular highlights along each capsule; thin cool rim light on the right edges; soft fill from below so the undersides show deep wine-red tones, never black. Rich red gradation from bright cherry to burgundy. Clean reflections, no hot spots, no noise, 8K detail, photorealistic.

Background: completely transparent background, PNG with alpha channel, isolated objects with clean anti-aliased edges. No background color, no floor, no cast shadow, no reflection, no drop shadow, no glow halo, no checkerboard pattern.

Strictly no text, no logos, no letters on the capsules, no hands, no props. Square 1:1, 2048 x 2048 px.
```

## 3 · Cápsula abriéndose con los ingredientes (la más "wow")

```
Use the attached photo only as a reference for the capsule's exact color and shape. Create an ultra-premium, hyper-realistic 3D product render of one two-piece hard gelatin capsule, deep crimson red (close to #C4161C), glossy and fully opaque, floating in mid-air and gently separating into its two halves along the seam, the cap and the body drifting apart about one capsule-width, both tilted diagonally at about 30 degrees.

From the opening between the two halves, a fine cloud of warm golden-beige powder bursts outward in a soft, elegant, frozen-in-time spiral, and suspended within the powder float a few small, perfectly realistic natural ingredients: two or three dark red grapes, a small slice of bitter orange, a couple of fresh green tea leaves, a few pumpkin seeds and two almonds. Everything is weightless, frozen at high speed, balanced around the capsule, with the capsule halves as the clear hero in the center. The whole composition fills about 80% of the canvas.

Lighting: luxury studio lighting. Soft key light from the upper left creating long specular highlights on the glossy red halves, thin cool rim light on the right edges, soft fill from below. Powder particles catch the light with a subtle sparkle. Rich color, crisp detail, 8K, photorealistic, premium supplement advertising aesthetic.

Background: completely transparent background, PNG with alpha channel, isolated composition with clean edges. No background color, no floor, no cast shadow, no reflection, no drop shadow, no checkerboard pattern.

Strictly no text, no logos, no hands, no packaging. Square 1:1, 2048 x 2048 px.
```

## Si querés la cápsula con el logo impreso
Agregá esta línea al prompt 1 o 2 (y sacá "no letters printed on the capsule"):

```
The capsule cap has the word "DRMEN" printed once in small, clean, white sans-serif capital letters, centered and wrapped naturally around the curvature of the capsule, like real pharmaceutical capsule printing ink.
```
La IA a veces escribe mal las letras. Si sale mal, pedí: "Fix the printed text so it reads exactly DRMEN".
