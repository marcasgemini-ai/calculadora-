# Bronzer: 12 estáticos con resultado + producto (1:1)

**Formato:** todos cuadrados **1:1 · 1080×1080**.
**La fórmula de cada anuncio:** **el resultado deseado** (una mujer argentina dorada, pareja y luminosa, en el verano soñado) **+ el frasco grande y nítido en primer plano + "BETACAROTENO"**.
**Sin logo:** ningún anuncio lleva el logo DR WOMAN. La marca se ve solo en la etiqueta del frasco.

---

## Antes de empezar

1. **Un chat nuevo por anuncio.** Adjuntá como **imagen 1 la foto real del frasco**: de frente, con fondo liso, buena luz y alta resolución. Si la foto es mala, el anuncio sale mal.
2. **Opcional, para que sea siempre la misma chica:** adjuntá como **imagen 2** la foto de la modelo que ya generaste para el Hero y agregá al final del prompt: `The woman is the same woman from image 2, same face and hair`.
3. Pegá el **BLOQUE DE MARCA** y, en el mismo mensaje, el prompt del anuncio.
4. **Generá 2 o 3 versiones** de cada uno y quedate con la mejor.
5. **Si la etiqueta del frasco sale deformada**, usá el método de 2 pasos:
   - **Paso 1:** al final del prompt agregá `Leave the bottle area empty: same scene, same lighting and shadow, without the bottle.`
   - **Paso 2:** en Canva, pegá el **PNG real del frasco sin fondo** en ese lugar, con una sombra suave. Queda 100 % fiel.
6. Correcciones rápidas:
   - Frasco: `Keep everything identical, only replace the bottle with the exact bottle from image 1, same size and position`.
   - Piel naranja o de plástico: `Keep everything identical, make her tan a more natural warm golden-bronze, real skin texture, not orange`.
   - Logo agregado: `Keep everything identical, remove any logo or brand name that is not printed on the bottle label`.
   - Texto mal escrito: `Keep everything identical, only fix the text so it reads exactly: "…"`.

### BLOQUE DE MARCA (pegalo antes de cada prompt)

```
BRAND SYSTEM — apply strictly.
Format: square 1:1, 1080x1080 px, premium Instagram/Facebook feed ad.
Every ad combines TWO heroes: (1) THE DESIRED RESULT — a real-looking Argentine woman with a warm, even, luminous golden-bronze tan in a dream summer moment, and (2) THE PRODUCT — the bottle from image 1, large, sharp and in the foreground. The viewer must instantly understand: "this bottle = that golden skin, without sun".
PRODUCT (image 1): reproduce the bottle EXACTLY: same shape, proportions, cap, colors, label layout, typography and printed brand. Never redraw, simplify, translate, re-letter or invent anything on the label; never add text to the bottle. Upright, front-facing or slight three-quarter view, label fully readable, tack sharp, realistic material and reflections, soft natural contact shadow so it physically sits in the scene — never floating, never pasted-looking. It must occupy at least 35% of the canvas height unless the prompt says otherwise.
NO LOGO: do not add any logo, wordmark, brand name or monogram anywhere in the design. The only branding allowed is what is already printed on the bottle label.
WOMEN: hyper-realistic, never CGI, never stock. Real-looking Argentine women with natural varied features; real skin texture (pores, freckles, peach fuzz, tiny imperfections); a warm, even, golden-bronze tan with a healthy natural sheen — never orange, never plastic, never over-smoothed, no beauty filter, natural bodies, no body reshaping, no tan lines. Candid, genuine emotion: joy, confidence, pleasure of seeing herself golden.
PHOTOGRAPHY: luxury beauty campaign quality (the level of Sol de Janeiro, Summer Fridays, Jacquemus and Vogue summer editorials): warm golden light, rich but natural cinematic grade (cream, honey, terracotta, bronze, turquoise water), slight film grain.
DESIGN: senior art director level: clear grid, 7% margins, perfect alignment, strong hierarchy (one headline, one small line, the seal), lots of breathing room, never cluttered, no clip-art, no random gradients.
Palette for graphics: warm cream #FBF6EF, sand #EAD9C4, honey gold #E3B57A, bronze #B46F34, deep cocoa #1E140F, bright gold #F2B544. Never pure white, never neon.
Typography: headlines in a high-contrast elegant display serif similar to Playfair Display (key words in italic); small lines and labels in a clean geometric sans-serif similar to Montserrat, uppercase with wide letter spacing.
SIGNATURE SEAL (mandatory, identical in every ad): a small rounded-rectangle badge in honey gold #E3B57A with a thin bronze #B46F34 outline and a tiny sun-drop icon, two lines in uppercase sans-serif deep cocoa #1E140F: "BETACAROTENO" (bold) / "FÓRMULA EXCLUSIVA" (small). It must not cover faces or the bottle label.
SPANISH TEXT: render ALL text exactly as written between quotes, Argentine Spanish, correct accents and punctuation (á é í ó ú ñ ¿ ¡), no extra words, no typos, no English in the image. Text never covers faces or the bottle label.
NEVER: sunburn, red skin, tanning beds, tanning oil, lying in direct midday sun, tan lines, logos, more text than requested.
```
